    RJ-11 Pinout
    ============

Pin1 - Slave TX, Master RX
Pin2 - VSYS
Pin3 - GND
Pin4 - NC

Notes:
1 - Could be used for backlighting, custom switches, etc
2 - Swap to I2C in a more modular design
